PROVENANCE WIKI - TESTING BUILD (Windows 10/11, 64-bit)
=======================================================

This build is for evaluation only. It is NOT code-signed, so Windows
SmartScreen may warn that it is from an unknown publisher. Do not share it
as a public download.

If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC":
  click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Installing does not need administrator rights. Provenance Wiki is added to
the Start menu. It opens in your web browser; a small "P" icon in the
system tray (bottom-right, possibly under the ^ arrow) lets you reopen it,
open your photos folder, or Quit.

On first launch, add your Anthropic and Voyage AI API keys. They are stored
in Windows Credential Manager.

Your archive is saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ProvenanceWiki. Uninstalling keeps
it unless you explicitly choose to delete it. Please test with sample
documents or COPIES of your material.
