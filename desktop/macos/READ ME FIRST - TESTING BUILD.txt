PROVENANCE WIKI — TESTING BUILD (macOS, Apple Silicon)
======================================================

This build is for evaluation only. It is NOT signed with an Apple Developer ID
and NOT notarized by Apple, so macOS will block it the first time you open it.
Do not share it as a public download.

Install
  1. Drag "Provenance Wiki (Testing)" into the Applications folder.
  2. Open it from Applications. macOS will say it cannot verify the app.
     Click "Done" (do NOT move it to the Trash).
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the message
     about "Provenance Wiki (Testing)" and click "Open Anyway". Enter your
     password if asked, then click "Open Anyway" again.
  You only need to do this once per version.

Use
  - The app opens in your web browser. A small "P" icon appears in the menu
    bar at the top of the screen: use it to reopen the app, open your photos
    folder, or Quit.
  - On first launch, add your Anthropic and Voyage AI API keys. They are
    stored in your macOS Keychain.
  - Please test with sample documents or COPIES of your material.

Your data
  ~/Library/Application Support/ProvenanceWiki   (archive and photographs)
  ~/Library/Logs/ProvenanceWiki                  (logs)
  Deleting the app does not delete your data.

Requirements: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
